Flip 7 Rules

Objective: First player to reach 200 total points wins!

Deck Breakdown:
• Numbers (0-12): Count equals copies (1x 0, 1x 1, 2x 2 ... up to 12x 12).
• Modifiers: +2, +3, +4, +5, +6, +7, +8, +9, +10, and x2 multiplier.
