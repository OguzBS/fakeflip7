Official Flip 7 Rules

Objective: First player to reach 200 total points wins the game!

Deck Breakdown (99 Cards Total):
• 79 Number Cards (0-12): Count equals copies (1x 0, 1x 1, 2x 2 ... up to 12x 12).
• 10 Modifier Cards: +2, +3, +4, +5, +6, +7, +8, +9, +10, and x2 multiplier.
• 10 Action Cards: Freeze (x3), Flip Three (x3), Second Chance (x4).

Player Turns: On your turn, choose to HIT (draw card) or STAY (lock points for the round).

Bust Rule: Drawing a duplicate number card busts your hand for 0 round points unless protected by Second Chance.

Second Chance Shield: Discards the duplicate card and the Second Chance shield card upon duplicate draw, allowing you to stay in the round.

Flip 7 Bonus: Accumulating 7 distinct number cards instantly ends the round and awards a +15 bonus point payout!
